About

Background and academic career

Yeongwook Yang received the master's degree in computer science education and the Ph.D. degree from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Korea University, Seoul, South Korea. He was a Research Professor with the Institute of Creative Information Education and the Human-Inspired AI Research Center, Korea University. He was also a Senior Researcher with the University of Tartu, Tartu, Estonia, and an Assistant Professor with the Division of Computer Engineering, Hanshin University. He joined the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Kangwon National University (formerly Gangneung-Wonju National University), as an Assistant Professor in 2024, and has been an Associate Professor there since October 2026, where he leads the Applied Machine Learning Lab (AML).

His research interests include information filtering, natural language processing, educational data mining, and deep learning.

He is also an active team member of the AIED–TLU research group (AI for Education) at the School of Digital Technologies, Tallinn University, Estonia, working on responsible, hybrid human–AI methods such as neural-symbolic AI for education.

Research Interests

Core areas of investigation

Information Filtering

Recommender systems, similarity modeling, and personalization for sparse and sequential data.

Natural Language Processing

Machine translation, grammatical error correction, and text classification for Korean and low-resource settings.

Educational Data Mining

Learner modeling, knowledge tracing, procrastination and engagement prediction, and adaptive educational games.

Deep Learning

Neural-symbolic methods, interpretable and trustworthy AI, and deep learning for education and language.
